Study reminder

Weather essentials

Weather instruments help us collect accurate information so we can understand the atmosphere, forecast future conditions, and make safe decisions.

🌡️ Temperature shows how hot or cold the air is.
🌧️ Rainfall tells us how much water falls in a period.
💨 Wind speed and direction show how air moves.
📈 Air pressure helps predict changing weather conditions.

Discussion

Class discussion questions

1. Why do we need different weather instruments?

Discuss how temperature, rainfall, wind, and pressure give us different pieces of weather information.

2. How can weather affect daily life?

Think about how people plan school, travel, farming, and safety using weather data.

3. Why is forecasting important?

Explain how meteorologists use measurements to warn people about storms and other changes.

4. How is climate different from weather?

Compare short-term conditions with long-term patterns in a place over many years.
